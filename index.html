Prpl‑Pair

Prpl‑Pair is a tactile/braille driver designed to give blind and low‑vision users structured, directional feedback on touchscreen devices. Instead of relying on audio cues alone, Prpl‑Pair translates interface actions into precise tactile pulses, patterns, and spatial signals.

  • Structured tactile output for navigation
  • Pattern‑based feedback for menus and controls
  • Adaptive pulse sequences for typing and gestures
  • Low‑latency response for real‑time interaction
  • Designed for mobile, tablet, and embedded systems

The goal: a universal tactile layer that makes modern touchscreens fully operable without sight — not through vibration spam, but through engineered tactile language.
